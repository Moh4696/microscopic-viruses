Reading the atlas

Choose a virus from the index. Select a numbered marker or a name in the part index to highlight the structure and read what it does. Labels follow visible structures as you rotate the model.

Use Cutaway to switch between the surface and interior. Drag to orbit, or focus the drawing and use the arrow keys. Use + and − to zoom.

Open Illness & symptoms for each virus’s disease profile and its public-health source. Models use illustrative colors and enlarged details. The arrangement of genetic material is schematic.
